Artificial Intelligence is widely used in real-world applications across multiple industries. In healthcare, AI systems assist doctors in diagnosing diseases, analyzing medical images, and predicting patient outcomes.

In the field of finance, AI is used for fraud detection, credit scoring, and algorithmic trading. Machine learning models analyze large amounts of financial data to identify patterns and make predictions.

AI is also transforming the transportation industry through self-driving cars and intelligent traffic management systems. These technologies aim to improve safety and reduce congestion on roads.

In e-commerce, recommendation systems powered by AI suggest products based on user behavior and preferences. This improves customer experience and increases sales.

Natural language processing enables chatbots and virtual assistants to understand and respond to human language. Applications like voice assistants and customer support bots rely heavily on AI technologies.

As AI continues to evolve, its applications are expected to grow, impacting almost every aspect of daily life.
